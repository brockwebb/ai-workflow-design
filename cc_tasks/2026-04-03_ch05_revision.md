## Evidence Package

### E1: Cross-Model Independence (Perplexity PQ-03)
Evidence supports that models from different vendor families show different agreement patterns, but the strongest evidence is about evaluation agreement, not general classification failure modes. One study found within-family models had stronger agreement while cross-vendor comparisons showed lower exact agreement. The claim "meaningfully different bias profiles" is stronger than the evidence supports. Defensible framing: "different agreement patterns and failure behavior" rather than "meaningfully different bias profiles."

### E2: Iteration Threshold (Perplexity PQ-04)
The "2-3 iterations" threshold is **not supported**. RefineBench reports that most models fail to consistently improve across iterations in self-refinement, with performance often flattening or degrading over five turns. The better-supported claim: self-refinement shows diminishing or negative returns over multiple rounds — no universal optimum exists.

### E3: Self-Refine ~20% (Perplexity PQ-01)
Confirmed as aggregate average across seven heterogeneous tasks. Not a uniform per-task gain.

### E4: TWIX Multipliers (Perplexity PQ-02)
Exact match confirmed: "520X faster and 3,786X cheaper." Source: ACM Digital Library page for the paper.

### E5: Self-Consistency Wording (Perplexity PQ-06)
"Majority voting over chain-of-thought samples" is fair shorthand. Canonical paper frames it as "sampling multiple diverse reasoning paths and selecting the most consistent answer." Current chapter text already uses the better framing.

### E6: Preference Leakage (Perplexity PQ-07)
Term confirmed in the literature. arXiv:2502.01534 defines it as contamination when generator and judge share model family or training lineage.

### E7: Ch 6 Bridge Content
Ch 6 does not contain "$15 versus $1,500" or any equivalent cost-comparison framing. Ch 6 covers: force multipliers, batch design, parallel architecture, model selection as engineering, infrastructure reinvention. The bridge must be rewritten to match.

**Revised text:**
