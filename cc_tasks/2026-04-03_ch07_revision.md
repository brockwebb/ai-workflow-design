## Fix 1: GPT-5 Temperature Claim — MUST FIX

**Source:** Synthesis Cluster 1 (conv 3), Perplexity PQ-01
**Severity:** HIGH — factual error

**Problem:** The sentence "When OpenAI moved to GPT-5, the temperature setting was removed" is factually wrong as stated. Perplexity confirms: temperature removal is well-documented for o-series reasoning models (o1, o3), and GPT-5 series models also do not support temperature, but the framing implies temperature was a GPT-4→GPT-5 removal when the pattern is broader (reasoning models and GPT-5 both dropped sampling controls).

**Fix:** Replace the GPT-5 sentence with something like: "When OpenAI introduced reasoning-focused models, sampling parameters like temperature were removed entirely. Practitioners who had invested time finding optimal temperature settings for specific tasks had that work invalidated by a model architecture change." This preserves the argument (parameter interfaces have a shelf life) with a verifiable, accurately scoped example. Do NOT name specific model series in body text (book rule: no specific company products named in body text). Frame generically: "When providers introduced reasoning-focused model architectures, sampling parameters like temperature were removed entirely."

**Verification:** After edit, confirm the paragraph still makes the parameter shelf-life argument without naming any specific model or provider.

## Fix 4: Prevention-Failure Recovery Paragraph

**Source:** Synthesis Cluster 7 (conv 2), Practitioner Stress Test P6
**Severity:** MEDIUM

**Problem:** The chapter teaches transaction-safe writes to prevent checkpoint corruption but does not address recovery when prevention fails (disk failure, filesystem corruption, manual deletion). Practitioner stress test P6 flagged this: the practitioner whose checkpoint IS corrupted gets no guidance.

**Fix:** Add one paragraph to the end of the Checkpoint and Recovery Architecture section, before the Recursive Problem section. Content should cover: if the checkpoint is corrupted or missing despite transaction-safe writes (hardware failure, manual deletion), the recovery path is the results file. Design the pipeline to write results independently of checkpoints so that a corrupted checkpoint costs only the delta between results and checkpoint, not the entire run. A corrupted checkpoint with intact results means you lose at most one checkpoint interval of work.
